RICE TERRECES

INTRODUCTION

The Banaue Rice Terraces are located in Banaue, Ifugao Province, in the northern Philippines. The place is famous for its beautiful and ancient rice terraces carved into the mountains. These terraces were created by the Ifugao people using traditional farming methods passed down through generations. What makes the Banaue Rice Terraces special is their amazing design, natural beauty, and importance to Ifugao culture and history. The terraces show the strong relationship between the Ifugao people, their land, and their traditional way of life. Tourists visit Banaue to enjoy the breathtaking mountain scenery, take photographs, hike, and learn about the culture and traditions of the Ifugao people. It is a wonderful destination for people who want to experience the beauty, history, and culture of the Philippine highlands.
