INTRODUCTION

I dream of visiting Canada, New York, and New Zealand because each place offers a different kind of beauty and adventure. In Canada, I want to see the breathtaking northern lights, the snowy mountains, and the kind people it is famous for. In New York, I want to experience the bright city lights, the tall skyscrapers, and the busy streets of Times Square that I only see in movies. And in New Zealand, I want to explore its peaceful nature, green hills, and stunning beaches that look like a paradise. Visiting these three places would be like seeing the best of both the city and nature in one amazing journey.
